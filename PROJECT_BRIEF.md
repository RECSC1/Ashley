# PROJECT_BRIEF.md

## Project Name

Ashley Smith Realtor Authority Website

## Project Type

Client website build through the Realtor Authority Website System by Real Estate Concierge Services Company LLC.

## Client

Ashley Smith is a Compass real estate agent based in Chapel Hill, North Carolina.

She owns her own brokerage under Compass and hired Emily Wyatt for a full-featured real estate website build.

## Primary Goal

Build a polished, conversion-focused, locally optimized website that positions Ashley as a trusted real estate authority in Chapel Hill and the greater Triangle.

The site should support visibility, credibility, lead capture, Google search, AI search, and future marketing support.

## Business Goals

This website should help Ashley:

1. Establish a stronger online presence
2. Build local authority in Chapel Hill and the Triangle
3. Support buyer, seller, relocation, luxury, investor, UNC-related, and move-up client inquiries
4. Make it easy for leads to contact her
5. Create clear consultation paths
6. Support future Google Business Profile work
7. Support local SEO
8. Present a polished Compass-affiliated brand presence
9. Avoid generic real estate website copy
10. Give Emily a strong reusable structure for future Realtor Authority Website builds

## Feature Safety Rules

Do not imply that a feature is fully functional unless it actually works.

Be careful with anything labeled:

- AI Home Search
- Map Search
- School Rating Search
- Home Valuation
- Property Alerts
- Buyer Dashboard
- Seller Dashboard
- IDX Search
- MLS Search
- Mortgage Calculator
- Community Landmarks
- Virtual Staging
- Saved Search
- Account Login
- Automated Alerts

If a feature is not truly connected to a backend, MLS, IDX, live data source, or automation system, label it professionally as informational, request-based, or concierge-style.

## Safe Copy Examples

Instead of:

“Search every home for sale instantly.”

Use:

“Tell Ashley what you’re looking for, and she’ll help you narrow the right homes, neighborhoods, and next steps.”

Instead of:

“Get an instant AI home valuation.”

Use:

“Request a personalized home value review based on your property, location, condition, and current market activity.”

Instead of:

“Sign up for automatic property alerts.”

Use:

“Share your search criteria, and Ashley can help you stay informed about homes that match your needs.”
